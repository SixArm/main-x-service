# Time-based analysis (TBA)

Time-based analysis (TBA) in the health care medical care pathway evaluates the
patient journey by measuring elapsed time through operational pathways.

Championed by figures like Dr. R.C. (Bob) Barker, this approach visualizes
service delivery to expose hidden waste and system bottlenecks without requiring
expensive new infrastructure.

## Core Principles of Time-Based Analysis

- Visualizing Flow: Maps the entire operational journey through the strict lens
  of time rather than departmental budgets.

- Identifying Constraints: Highlights specific blockages, idle waiting times,
  and redundant handoffs that prolong the patient experience.

- Staff-Led Design: Empowers frontline healthcare workers—rather than external management consultants—to redesign workflows.

## Implementation and Impact

- Maximizing Assets: Asserts that existing capital, equipment, and staff
  infrastructure possess massive untapped capacity if scheduled and sequenced
  efficiently.
 
- Streamlining Pathways: Focuses on high-volume outpatients and clinical
  departments to reduce repeat visits and bottlenecks.
 
- Cultural Shift: Encourages multidisciplinary engagement where staff actively
  question non-value-adding administrative routines.
